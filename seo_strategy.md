# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public-facing pages and the unauthenticated application shell.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated workflows, dashboards, and internal API endpoints except where their shared shell affects public crawlability.
- The design-only mockup sandbox.

## Target audience
- OSDU platform users and energy-data professionals exploring records, schemas, legal tags, and reservoir data.

## Primary keywords
- OSDU Explorer
- OSDU records
- OSDU schemas
- OSDU platform data explorer

## Crawler assumptions
- Public landing or product information should be visible in initial HTML to search, social-preview, and AI crawlers.

## Dismissed categories
- None.
